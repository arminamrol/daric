## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, the CLA check comments on it. Reply on the pull request with exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username, the date and the pull request are recorded in `signatures/cla.json` on the `cla-signatures` branch. You sign once; it covers all your later Contributions. Pull requests cannot be merged until every commit author has signed.
