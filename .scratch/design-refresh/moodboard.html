Style tile · Colour

Gold and navy, kept and refined

The brand ramps stay as they are. The theme roles change: the light ground moves from warm cream to a navy-tinted porcelain so gold stands out, the dark theme gets a third surface step for raised cards, and there are new roles for Transfer, warning, info and strong borders. Each pair below shows its contrast ratio, calculated live on this page.

Style tile · Type

Vazirmatn for Persian, Inter for English

These are the two faces Daric already ships. The scale gets three display sizes for balances and totals. Persian keeps roughly 1.6× leading for body text so dots and descenders have room. Amounts always use tabular digits, Persian digits in Persian.

Style tile · Shape & depth

Soft corners, low shadows, a 4-point grid

Radii get bigger as objects get bigger: chips are fully round, inputs and buttons 12, cards 18, sheets 28. Light mode uses soft shadows for elevation. Dark mode adds a lighter surface step and an inner top highlight, because shadows barely show on navy.

Style tile · Components

Core components

The pieces every screen is built from, shown in both themes. Income, Expense and Transfer differ in sign, icon and colour, so they never rely on colour alone. Budget states add an icon, a word and a striped fill when over the limit.

The same screen in light and dark, Persian and English, at phone width. From the top: Workspace switcher, Net Worth with this month’s change, this Period’s Income and Expense, Budgets close to their limit, a Due Occurrence to confirm, and recent Transactions. The figures are examples.

Navigation

Which four sections get a tab?

Home, Transactions and More are fixed. Budgets and Insights compete for the last slot next to the “+”. The section that loses is reached from a Home card and from More.
