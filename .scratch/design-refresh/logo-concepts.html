Daric design refresh · ticket DR-02 Chosen: Breaking rim

Four ways to strike the coin

The Daric was the gold coin of the Achaemenid kings, stamped with a kneeling archer drawing his bow. Each concept below keeps the gold disc and borrows one part of that archer, simplified until it still reads in a 16-pixel browser tab.

All four at 16px, actual size, in a tab bar
