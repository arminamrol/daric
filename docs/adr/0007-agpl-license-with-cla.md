# AGPL-3.0 with a CLA; self-hosting is free, the hosted service is paid

Daric is published under AGPL-3.0 so anyone can read, audit and self-host it, which matters for an app that holds people's finances, while a competitor who runs a modified copy as a service must publish their changes. Every contributor signs a CLA granting the maintainer the right to relicense their contribution; without it, a single outside PR would stop us shipping builds under other terms (e.g. to app stores whose terms clash with the GPL) or selling a commercial licence. Revenue comes from the hosted service, not from code: a self-hosted instance runs with billing disabled and every Entitlement unlimited (ADR-0008), and the paid features are not hidden in a separately licensed folder, because gate code inside an AGPL repo is trivially removed. We rejected MIT/Apache (a competitor could host it with no obligation) and source-available licences like FSL/BSL (not open source, smaller community).
