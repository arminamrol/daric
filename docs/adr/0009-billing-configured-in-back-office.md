# Billing is configured by the Operator in the Back Office, not by env

ADR-0008 priced Plus from deployment config. Prices, Tier Discounts, Promotions, Trial and Grace days, each Plan's limits, the enabled Payment Methods and `PAYMENT_LINK_MODE` now live in the database, versioned and audit-logged, and the Operator changes them from the Back Office without a redeploy; env only seeds them on first boot. Env keeps what belongs to the deployment: `BILLING_ENABLED` and every Payment Provider's credentials, which never enter the database or the Back Office. Plans are rows rather than a fixed enum so a Business plan can be added later, but only Free and Plus exist and there is no switching between paid Plans. A change applies to Payments started and Users signed up afterwards; a Payment keeps the price it was started at.

Because a gateway contract may be late or lost, card-to-card is a Payment Method too: the User reports the transfer and the Payment is Awaiting Review until the Operator approves or rejects it. Operators are Users flagged by a CLI script, see only account and billing data (never Workspace data, which RLS keeps out of reach), and re-enter their password before every write.
